FinOps FinOps is a portmanteau of Finance and DevOps, emphasizing communication and collaboration between business and engineering teams. At its core, FinOps is a cultural practice. It enables teams to manage cloud costs by ensuring that everyone takes ownership of their usage, supported by a central best-practices group. Cross-functional teams across Engineering, Finance, and Product work together to accelerate product delivery while maintaining financial control and predictability. FinOps Principles FinOps Principles act as the north star for guiding activities within a FinOps practice.
